# Marketing Analytics: Customer Purchase Prediction

## Overview

This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior and develops a multiple linear regression model to predict purchase amount based on demographic and behavioral characteristics.

The analysis combines exploratory data analysis, statistical analysis, feature engineering, and regression modeling to identify the factors most associated with customer spending.

## Business Question

**What customer characteristics are most strongly associated with purchase amount, and how can these insights support marketing decisions?**

## Objectives

- Explore customer demographics and purchasing behavior.
- Identify relationships between customer characteristics and purchase amount.
- Develop and evaluate a multiple linear regression model.
- Translate analytical findings into actionable marketing insights.

## Analysis

The project includes:

- Exploratory data analysis
- Correlation analysis
- Feature engineering
- Multiple linear regression
- Model evaluation
- Statistical interpretation
- Business recommendations

## Key Insights

The analysis identified meaningful relationships between customer characteristics and purchase amount and highlighted the variables that contribute most to explaining differences in customer spending.

The regression model provides a quantitative basis for understanding purchasing behavior and can support customer segmentation and targeted marketing strategies.

## Business Recommendations

The findings can support marketing teams in:

- Identifying customer characteristics associated with higher purchase amounts.
- Improving customer segmentation strategies.
- Prioritizing customer groups for targeted marketing campaigns.
- Using predictive analytics to support data-driven marketing decisions.
